Your two options

HSG Housing Office or a student sublet?

HSG runs its own Housing Office for incoming students, and it's a good option. Here's how it compares, so you can pick what suits you. Students also post rooms on the SHSG Marketplace, which is open to everyone.

HSG Housing Office
  • CHF 700–1,050 per month, all bills included
  • CHF 250 non-refundable application fee
  • No deposit
  • Fixed contract dates: 1 Feb – 31 May or 1 Aug – 31 Dec
  • Mostly living with other exchange students
  • First come, first served, so apply early

Arrival checklist

What to sort out, and when.

The short version of what every exchange student wishes they'd known before arriving in St. Gallen. Official steps first, tips from former exchange students further down.

8 to 12 weeks before

Before you fly

  • 1
    Secure a room. Good sublets and HSG Housing rooms both go early.
  • 2
    From a European partner university? Apply for the SEMP grant in Mobility-Online before the deadline.
  • 3
    Bring your European Health Insurance Card (EHIC), or arrange student health insurance if you're from outside the EU/EFTA.
Within 14 days

Register in the city

  • 4
    Go to the residents' office (Bevölkerungsdienste) at the city hall, Poststrasse 28, 1st floor, next to the main station.
  • 5
    Bring your passport or ID, a copy of your rental agreement (our sublease template works) and the printed Form R from HSG.
  • 6
    Residence permit L: CHF 71 for EU/EFTA citizens, CHF 148 for most others. Upload the stamped Form R to Mobility-Online afterwards.
Within 3 months

Settling in

  • 7
    Health insurance: even with an EHIC you must apply for an exemption at the city within 3 months.
  • 8
    Get a Swiss eSIM and sort out paying in CHF and your bus pass.
  • 9
    Explore clubs, sports and tools on the sg/unihub dashboard.

Based on HSG's Form R and health insurance fact sheet for incoming students (as of 2026). Fees and rules can change, so always check the latest HSG info in Mobility-Online.

From former exchange students

Tips we wish someone had told us.

Collected from students who did their exchange at HSG. Personal experience, not official rules.

Bidding for courses

Bidding is the hardest part for most. For compulsory core courses like microeconomics or computer science you usually get in anyway, so save your points for the elective areas, where places are competitive.

Spread your schedule

Packing all courses into Monday to Wednesday sounds great for long weekends. Many found it exhausting after a few weeks, so spreading courses over the week is often the better choice.

Do the readings

HSG courses expect you to prepare before class. Reading the assigned material makes a big difference, and it's easy to underestimate in the first weeks.

SEMP grant

Switzerland's version of the Erasmus grant for students from European partner universities, roughly CHF 380 to 440 per month. Apply in Mobility-Online and keep an eye on the deadlines.

FAQ

Good questions.

How do exchange students find a room in St. Gallen?

Many HSG students go abroad for a semester themselves and sublet their furnished room in the meantime, which fits an exchange semester perfectly. Browse these sublets here for free and message the lister directly.

How do I know a listing is real?

Listers verify their HSG student email before they can post, or the listing was added by sg/unihub with the owner's consent. We don't check listings and aren't a party to any rental agreement, so: never pay before you've viewed the room (in person, by video call, or via Outvisit), and always sign a written sublease.

Does it cost anything to find a room?

No. Browsing and messaging listers is free, and we never charge to see contact details. Listers can optionally pay to feature their listing; that's how the platform pays for itself.

Is subletting allowed in Switzerland?

Generally yes, but the main tenant needs the landlord's consent. Listers confirm this when they post, and our fillable sublease template (German/English) includes it.

Do exchange students need Swiss health insurance?

Health insurance is compulsory for stays longer than three months. Students from the EU/EFTA or UK with a valid European Health Insurance Card (EHIC/GHIC) who don't work in Switzerland are usually exempted, but they still have to apply for the exemption at the city within three months of arrival. HSG's fact sheet for incoming students explains the process.

How do I register in St. Gallen?

Within 14 days of arriving, go to the residents' office at the city hall (Poststrasse 28, next to the main station) with your passport or ID, a copy of your rental agreement and HSG's printed Form R. The residence permit costs CHF 71 for EU/EFTA citizens and CHF 148 for most others. Afterwards, upload the stamped form to Mobility-Online.

How do I remove my listing once the room is taken?

Click My listing on the housing board and verify with the same HSG email you used to post. You can then mark the room as rented (it disappears from the board) or delete the listing completely. The link is also in every email you get about your room.

Is this an official HSG offering?

No. sg/unihub is an independent project by HSG students and isn't operated by the University of St. Gallen.
